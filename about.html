About Our Portal

A simple digital platform designed for BUP students.

BUP Student Service Portal

BUP Student Service Portal is a web-based platform designed to make student services and information easier to access. Students can explore different services, check upcoming events, and register for university activities.

The goal of this project is to create a simple, responsive and user-friendly interface using Bootstrap and JavaScript.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide students with an easy and organized platform for accessing university services.

🚀 Our Vision

To create a connected digital environment where students can easily access information and participate in university activities.
